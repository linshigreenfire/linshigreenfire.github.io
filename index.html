About me

I am motivated by a simple question: how can we make better decisions when environmental systems are complex, uncertain, and deeply connected?

I am a senior sustainability scientist at Amazon Lab126. My work applies life cycle assessment, industrial ecology, data science, and systems thinking to the environmental challenges of technology.

I earned my PhD from Stanford’s Emmett Interdisciplinary Program in Environment and Resources, where I studied supply chain sustainability in the information and communication technologies industry. Earlier in my career, I worked across conservation fieldwork, landscape ecology, and environmental engineering.

I care about research that is analytically rigorous, useful in practice, and communicated clearly enough to change decisions.
