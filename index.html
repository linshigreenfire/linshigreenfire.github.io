Research agenda

Connecting environmental science with the frontier of technology.

My research moves between product-level measurement, global value chains, digital behavior, and AI governance. The common thread is decision-making under complex system constraints.

01 / RESPONSIBLE AI

Risk-aware generative AI for sustainability

Structured methods to identify, test, evaluate, and mitigate misinformation, data leakage, and other deployment risks in sustainability-focused AI systems.

02 / DIGITAL SYSTEMS

Climate impacts beyond the device

New ways to understand the emissions monitored, induced, or enabled by digital behavior—not only the embodied footprint of hardware.

03 / INDUSTRIAL ECOLOGY

Low-carbon technology value chains

Life cycle assessment and systems methods for electronics, semiconductors, circularity, product durability, and supply-chain decarbonization.

About

Scientist, systems thinker, and translator across disciplines.

I am a senior sustainability scientist at Amazon Lab126, where I work on environmental challenges at the intersection of consumer technology, supply chains, life cycle assessment, and responsible AI.

I earned my PhD from Stanford’s Emmett Interdisciplinary Program in Environment and Resources. My earlier work spans environmental engineering, landscape ecology, conservation fieldwork, industrial ecology, and technology-sector sustainability.

I am most energized by research that is scientifically rigorous, useful in practice, and clear enough to change how decisions are made.
